# Recorded evidence

`run-summary.json` contains aggregate outcomes from actual pretrained inference,
chronological evaluation, three-way adaptation, Chronos-2, MCP and browser runs.
Desktop/mobile screenshots remain local under `output/browser/`; automatic
approval review rejected publishing screenshots and requested code/text only.

Raw input candles, full trajectory exports, training checkpoints and private
application tokens remain in ignored local directories (`data/`, `output/`,
`.cache/`). Complete reports are under `output/real-run/` on the development machine.
The small recorded runs verify execution; they do not establish research quality,
forecast-skill stability, investment performance or adaptation gains.
