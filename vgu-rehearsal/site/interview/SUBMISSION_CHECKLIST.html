Generic rehearsal and preparation checklist

This template contains no appointment details, private records or administrative contact information. Check arrangements privately with the relevant organizer. Every box starts unchecked; generating files does not perform these human actions.

Rehearsal

  • Deliver the lecture aloud with a timer; complete the planned 30 minutes and keep Q&A separate.
  • Deliver the research talk aloud with a timer; complete the planned 20 minutes and keep Q&A separate.
  • Explain a fresh numerical attention example without reading the answer.
  • Diagnose a changed shape, mask or shifted-target example and explain the correction.
  • State the population and metric for each cited research result.
  • Distinguish published collaborative results from personal contribution and proposed work.
  • Explain one baseline, leakage risk, actual error and limitation.
  • Ask a listener for feedback and record what you changed afterwards.

Equipment and material selection

  • Open the selected PDF/PPTX on the presentation machine and check its appearance.
  • Check presenter notes, planned timings and the intended display arrangement.
  • Confirm the project interpreter/kernel and inspect actual notebook outputs.
  • Keep the PDF, calculation cards and saved outputs available offline.
  • Select the learner handout separately from instructor answers when assessing students.
  • Check selected attachments for unintended private information and unsupported claims.

Personal and administrative actions

  • Confirm the actual audience, topic, schedule, format and deadlines with the organizer.
  • Verify any personal-history statement against your own records before saying it.
  • Complete any required administrative forms and personal confirmations outside this repository.
  • Submit only the requested final materials through the intended channel yourself.
  • Retain your own record of the files actually submitted and the actual submission time.

Software checks establish properties of files and tested code paths. They do not establish teaching effectiveness, a qualification decision, a personal contribution or completed submission.
